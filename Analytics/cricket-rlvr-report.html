RLVR on 32 Vera Rubin GPUs: what the run did and how well the GPUs were used

Qwen3.8-27B was trained with reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards on T20 cricket questions (Rhea job 39000, 13 Nov 2026). The model answered questions itself and was rewarded only for a correct final number, with a small bonus for shorter answers.
