Qwen3.6-35B-A3B with RLVR: before and after, and how the 32 GPUs were used

Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, a mixture-of-experts model, was trained with reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards on T20 cricket questions (Rhea job 40421, 13 Nov 2026). The model answered questions itself and was rewarded only for a correct final number, with a bonus for shorter answers.
