Two RLVR runs on Rhea, end to end

Two models were trained with reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) on the same T20 cricket questions, on the same 32 Vera Rubin GPUs, on 13 November 2026. This page covers what was done, what each model learned, the before and after benchmark, how the GPUs were used, and what to change next time.

How RLVR worked here

No worked solutions were used as training targets. The model answered questions itself, and a checker compared its final number with the exact answer.

The two recipes

Same method and dataset; the settings that differ are what the second model needed.
